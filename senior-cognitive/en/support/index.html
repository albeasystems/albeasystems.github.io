Help and contact

For older people, relatives and care centres

Write to us

For any question, problem or suggestion: albeasystems@gmail.com

So we can help you sooner, tell us which iPhone or iPad you have, which iOS version it runs, the app version (at the very bottom of Settings) and what happened.

What do I need to use it?

An iPhone or an iPad with iOS 17 or later. The app is in Spanish and English. You do not need to create an account.

The Plus subscription

How do I subscribe?

In the app, tap Settings and then Plus. The App Store shows the price in your country.

How do I cancel?

In your iPhone Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions and Senior Cognitive Plus. You can also do it from Settings → Plus → Manage or cancel. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel, at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I changed phone or reinstalled the app and Plus is missing

Open Settings → Plus and tap Restore purchases. You must be signed in with the same Apple ID you paid with.

Can a relative pay for it?

Yes. With Apple's Family Sharing, the person who organises the family group can pay for it and the other members use it.

Family: following progress from another phone

How do I invite a relative?

In Settings → Your family, tap Invite a relative and send the link by Messages, WhatsApp or email. You need to be signed in to iCloud on your device. Inviting is a Plus feature; following someone who has invited you is free.

My relative cannot see my progress

Check that they opened the link on their iPhone or iPad with the app installed, that iCloud is on and that there is an internet connection. If opening the link does not work, they can copy the invitation and tap Paste the invitation in the app. Data is updated when each exercise is finished; it can take a few minutes to appear.

How do I stop sharing?

In Settings → Your family → Stop sharing. The link stops working and the copy is deleted.

Seeing and hearing better

In Settings you can change the text size, contrast, animations, sounds and music, the voice that reads the instructions and the language. Changes apply as soon as you save them.

My data

Where are my results kept?

On your own device. If you change phone, the easiest way to keep them is to restore the new one from your iCloud or computer backup. If you uninstall the app without a backup, they are lost.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. If you were sharing with family, tap Stop sharing first.

You can read every detail in the privacy policy.

If something does not work

Close the app completely and open it again. Check that you have the latest version from the App Store. If the problem continues, write to us with the details above and, if you can, attach a screenshot.
