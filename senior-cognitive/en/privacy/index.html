Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short

  • Senior Cognitive does not ask for an account, an email address or a password.
  • What you do in the app is kept on your iPhone or iPad.
  • It only leaves your device if you invite a relative to follow you, or if you send a report yourself.
  • The free version shows one small ad at the bottom of the main screens. With Plus there are no ads.

1. Who is responsible

Albea Systems. For any question about this policy, write to albeasystems@gmail.com.

2. What the app keeps on your device

The app remembers, in a file inside your own iPhone or iPad:

  • the name or nickname you type when you start (optional);
  • the kinds of games you prefer and your level;
  • your results, your exercise history and your slips;
  • your settings: text size, language, sounds, voice, contrast and reminder.

We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. If you have iCloud or computer backup switched on, it is part of that backup, which Apple manages.

The app does not use your location, your contacts, your microphone or your camera, and it has no analytics tools of its own.

3. Your photos (Plus)

With Plus you can use your own photos in the puzzle and in the pairs game. You pick them one by one. They stay on your device and are not uploaded to any server.

4. Family: following your progress from another phone

This only happens if you decide to invite a relative (inviting is a Plus feature; following someone who invites you is free).

  • A copy of your progress (name, level, results, exercise history and slips) is stored in your own iCloud and shared read-only with whoever receives your link.
  • That copy lives in iCloud, which Apple manages. We do not receive it and cannot see it.
  • Each relative uses their own Apple ID. No health information is shared: only what you see on the app's screens.
  • You can stop at any time in Settings → Your family → Stop sharing. The link stops working and the copy is deleted.

5. Reports and messages you send

The PDF report and the family summary are prepared on your device. They only leave it if you send them with the iOS share button, to the person or app you choose.

6. Reminders and voice

The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your own device: it does not go through any server. To switch it on, iOS asks your permission to send notifications, and you can withdraw it at any time in the iPhone Settings. Spoken instructions use the voice that already comes with your iPhone or iPad.

7. Plus subscription

Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple. We never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether Plus is active. Apple gives us sales reports in aggregate, without personal data.

8. Ads in the free version

The free version shows a banner ad on the four main screens. It never appears inside a game, the daily training or the family screen. With Plus no ads are shown.

Ads are served by Unity LevelPlay, which shares each ad slot between several ad networks (Unity Ads and Google AdMob). To show and measure an ad, these companies may receive technical data from the device, such as the IP address, the model, the language and whether the ad was tapped. Each one handles that data under its own privacy policy.

  • The app does not show the iOS prompt that asks permission to track you across apps, so the networks do not receive your device's advertising identifier and ads are not personalised with it.
  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's consent form appears before any ad is shown, where you can accept or decline. You can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy (it appears in these countries).

9. Children

Senior Cognitive is made for older people and their families. It is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Health

Senior Cognitive is a tool to exercise the mind with games. It is not a medical product, gives no diagnoses and does not replace a health professional.

11. Your rights

Because we do not keep your data on our servers, you stay in control: delete the app to remove the data on the device, stop sharing with family to delete the iCloud copy, and withdraw permissions in iOS Settings. If you want to access, correct or delete anything else, or you have a complaint, write to albeasystems@gmail.com. You can also contact the data protection authority in your country.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change something that affects you, we will update this page and the date above. Important changes will also be announced inside the app.
