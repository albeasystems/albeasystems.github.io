Six different exercises every day

Today’s plan is prepared automatically, with games of at least four different types, and stays the same all day. Tomorrow it will be different.

Between exercises, a screen congratulates the person and moves on by itself after 10 seconds. Nobody has to go back.

  • Every exercise can be listened to: one button reads it aloud, in Spanish or English.
  • You can pause, leave and carry on later without losing what was done.
  • The important buttons (Next, Check, Hint) are always at the bottom, in view.

Family module: see how it’s going, from your phone

Whoever cares, even from far away, can see whether today’s training is done, how it went and what is harder, without having to ask.

Whoever follows pays nothing. The person using the app needs Plus to send the invitation.

  1. The person invites. They tap “Invite a relative” in Settings and send the link by WhatsApp, message or email.
  2. The relative opens the link with the same app installed on their phone.
  3. They see the data on their phone: today, the last 7 days, each exercise with its slips, and the progress.

The person decides. The copy is read-only, goes through their iCloud (no new accounts) and they can stop sharing whenever they like.

Thirteen games and seven challenges, more than 1,100 different rounds

Each game comes with its own themes and rules: fruit, the bathroom, the farm, counting in 7s, putting the steps of making tea in order. With six games a day, none comes back for a month and a half. The ones marked Plus are part of the subscription.

What counts is finishing

Each exercise is worth 100 points: 80 for the answers, 10 for finishing and 10 for improving. Time does not count.

Moving up is celebrated

When the person moves up an animal there is a party. If moving down makes sense, it happens without fanfare.

Hints are counted

Hints are few and you always see how many are left: “Hint (3 left)”.

A week of training

Every day brings a new plan of six exercises. The app adds up the days trained, the points and the minutes of the week, and keeps them in the calendar.

  • One rest day a week does not break the streak.

Questions

Is it a medical treatment or assessment?

No. Senior Cognitive is a tool to exercise the mind with games. It does not diagnose or treat anything and does not replace the advice of a health professional.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There are no sign-ups or accounts: the data stays on the person’s device.

Who can see the data?

Only the person, unless they invite a relative. Then a read-only copy is shared through their iCloud, and they can stop sharing whenever they want.

Are there ads?

No. Senior Cognitive has no ads: not in the games and not on any other screen, with or without Plus.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, in portrait and landscape. It is available in Spanish and English, with spoken instructions in both.

What happens to my own photos?

With Plus you can use up to 12 of your own photos in the puzzle and pairs. They stay on the phone and are not uploaded anywhere.
