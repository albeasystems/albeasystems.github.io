Support

Answers to common questions. If you do not find yours, write to us.

How do I connect my Google account?

Open Settings in the app, tap Sign in with Google, choose the account that has AdMob or AdSense and accept read-only access.

Why does the app say it is showing sample data?

Until you connect Google, the app shows sample numbers so you can look around. Connect your account in Settings to see your own earnings.

Why do the numbers differ from Google's console?

Google reports estimates and finalises them at month end. Dates follow your account's reporting time zone and the currency can differ. The payment balance is an estimate made by the app.

The app says my Google session expired.

Google sessions can expire. Pull down to refresh. If it still fails, sign out and sign in again from Settings.

How do I sign out or remove access?

In Settings, tap Sign out. You can also revoke access from your Google account permissions.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you bought Pro with.
