Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The free version shows ads. A one-time Pro purchase removes them and adds alerts and PDF or CSV reports. A rewarded video also gives you one hour without ads.

Do I need an account with you?

No. There is no account and no server of ours. You sign in with Google and the app talks to Google directly from your phone.

What can the app see in my Google account?

Read-only access to your AdMob and AdSense reports. It cannot change anything, see payment details or read your email.

Are the numbers final?

No. AdMob and AdSense report estimated earnings and Google finalises them at the end of the month. The payment balance is an estimate made by the app, because Google does not provide it.

Can I try it without connecting Google?

Yes. The app opens with sample data so you can look around first.

Is this an official Google app?

No. It is an independent app by Albea Systems and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google.
