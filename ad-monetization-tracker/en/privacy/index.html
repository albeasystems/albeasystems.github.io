Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains how Albea Systems handles information when you use the Ad Monetization Tracker app for iPhone and iPad. The app shows the ad earnings of your own Google AdMob and Google AdSense accounts.

In short

  • We have no account system and no server for your data. We do not receive your earnings or your Google data.
  • You sign in with Google. The app gets read-only access to your AdMob and AdSense reports, directly from Google to your device.
  • Your Google sign-in is stored in the iPhone Keychain, on your device only.
  • The free version shows ads from third-party ad networks, which have their own data practices. You choose whether to allow tracking.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal identifier. We do not run a backend, and the app has no analytics or crash reporting of our own.

Google account access

When you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks for these permissions:

  • admob.readonly: view your AdMob reports (earnings, impressions, eCPM, clicks, by app, country, format and ad unit).
  • adsense.readonly: view your AdSense reports (the same, by site and country).
  • openid, email and profile: show which Google account is connected, on your device only.

The app cannot change anything in your accounts, see payment details or read your email. Reports are requested from Google by the app on your device, shown to you, and not sent to us or to anyone else. You may grant only one of the two reporting permissions.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information stored on your device

  • Your Google sign-in (a refresh token) and the connected email address, in the iOS Keychain.
  • Preferences: language, display currency, monthly goal, payment threshold, alert rules and whether you own Pro.
  • Widget numbers (today's and this month's earnings) in an App Group shared only with the app's own widgets.
  • Reports (PDF or CSV) are created on your device. They leave it only if you share them.

You can remove your Google sign-in at any time with Settings > Google account > Sign out, and revoke access from your Google account permissions. Deleting an app does not always delete its Keychain items on iOS, so sign out before you uninstall.

Connections the app makes

  • Google (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com, admob.googleapis.com, adsense.googleapis.com): sign-in and your reports.
  • frankfurter.dev: public exchange rates, used only to convert the built-in sample data. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Apple: App Store purchases, ratings and notifications.
  • Ad networks (see below): only in the free version.

Advertising and tracking

The free version shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through an ad mediation service (Unity LevelPlay) that works with networks such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network. These networks may use your device's advertising identifier to show relevant ads and measure them, only if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested, and you can reopen it from Settings > Privacy choices (ads).

Buying Pro (a one-time purchase) removes all ads. A rewarded video removes ads for one hour.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13, or the minimum age in your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
