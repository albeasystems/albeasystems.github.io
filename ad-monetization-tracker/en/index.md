Read-only, straight from Google to your phone: today, this month and your estimated next payout. No server of ours.

App Store: https://apps.apple.com/

## Revenue, eCPM, countries and formats in one scroll

The numbers you open the consoles for, sized for a glance on your phone.

- Countries: Where your revenue comes from, on a map and in a ranked list.
- Ad formats: Banner, interstitial, rewarded and more, with eCPM and match rate.
- Apps and sites: Each app and site with revenue, impressions, eCPM and trend.
- Calendar and goal: Every day at a glance, your best days and progress to a monthly goal.
- Reports: A PDF and two CSV files for any period. Part of Pro.

## Your earnings on the home screen

Two widgets keep the number in view without opening the app.

- Ad earnings: Today, this month and the last seven days. Small, medium and lock screen.
- Goal and payment: Progress to your monthly goal and the estimated balance towards the payment threshold.

## Straight from Google to your phone

No server of ours sits in between, so your earnings are never sent to us.

- You sign in with Google and grant read-only access.
- Your phone asks Google for the reports and shows them to you.
- The sign-in stays in the iPhone Keychain. Sign out removes it.

## Questions

### Is it really free?

Yes. The free version shows ads. A one-time Pro purchase removes them and adds alerts and PDF or CSV reports. A rewarded video also gives you one hour without ads.

### Do I need an account with you?

No. There is no account and no server of ours. You sign in with Google and the app talks to Google directly from your phone.

### What can the app see in my Google account?

Read-only access to your AdMob and AdSense reports. It cannot change anything, see payment details or read your email.

### Are the numbers final?

No. AdMob and AdSense report estimated earnings and Google finalises them at the end of the month. The payment balance is an estimate made by the app, because Google does not provide it.

### Can I try it without connecting Google?

Yes. The app opens with sample data so you can look around first.

### Is this an official Google app?

No. It is an independent app by Albea Systems and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google.
