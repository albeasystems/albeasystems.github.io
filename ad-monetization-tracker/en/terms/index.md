## What the app does

The app shows earnings and related figures from your own Google AdMob and Google AdSense accounts, read-only. It lets you set a monthly goal and see an estimate of your payment balance. It can also run on built-in sample data.

## Estimates, not advice

Google reports estimated earnings and finalises them at the end of the month. The payment balance and payment date shown in the app are estimates made by the app, because Google's APIs do not provide them. Figures can differ from Google's own reports because of time zones, currencies, delays and rounding. Nothing in the app is financial, tax or accounting advice. Check Google's own reports before you rely on a number.
